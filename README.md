# 💳 Credit Card Customer & Transaction Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
Developed an interactive **Credit Card Customer and Transaction Analysis Dashboard** using Power BI to analyze customer demographics, credit card transactions, revenue generation, and spending behavior. This project transforms customer and transaction data into meaningful insights that support business decision-making and performance monitoring.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze credit card revenue, interest earned, and transaction performance.
- Understand customer demographics, income groups, and employment categories.
- Identify spending patterns across card categories and expenditure types.
- Monitor weekly and quarterly revenue trends.
- Explore customer satisfaction and credit card usage behavior.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV:** Dataset handling and preparation.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** KPI calculations and analytical measures.
- **Data Analytics:** Trend analysis and business insights.

## 📊 Key Dashboard Features
- # Customer Report: Revenue by age group, income group, gender, marital status, education, occupation, and state.
- # Transaction Report: Revenue by card category, expenditure type, education, occupation, and chip usage.
- # KPI Cards: Revenue, interest earned, transaction amount, transaction count, income, and customer satisfaction score.
- # Trend Analysis: Weekly and quarterly revenue and transaction volume comparisons.
- # Interactive Filters: Quarter, week, card category, gender, and other customer segments.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Total revenue is approximately **55M**, with interest earned of around **7.84M**.
- The Blue card category contributes the largest share of card revenue.
- Swipe transactions account for a significant portion of transaction revenue.
- Businessmen and graduates represent important customer segments.
- Weekly and quarterly comparisons help identify changes in revenue and transaction activity.

## 💡 Skills Demonstrated
Data Cleaning, Data Modeling, DAX, Power BI Dashboard Development, KPI Tracking, Data Visualization, Business Intelligence, and Customer Segmentation.

## 🚀 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how Power BI can convert raw customer and transaction datasets into interactive dashboards, helping businesses understand customer behavior, evaluate financial performance, and make data-driven decisions.
